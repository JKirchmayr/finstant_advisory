Software Carve-Out

tecvia

Acquired by Unigestion

2023Carve-OutSoftware

In June 2023, Unigestion Private Equity acquired Springer Nature’s mobility education and training business via a carve-out, consolidating the assets into the newly formed TecVia Group — a leading pan-European provider of content and software for driving schools and commercial driver training, headquartered in Munich.

Key facts
  • 100% carve-out of Springer Nature’s mobility education & training unit
  • Operations across Germany, France, Spain, Austria and Switzerland
  • Brands include Verlag Heinrich Vogel, Codes Rousseau and Etrasa
  • Full-stack offering: ERP, teaching software, content and driving simulators

Healthcare Succession

Dovida

Acquired by Unigestion

2021SuccessionHealthcare

In 2021, Unigestion Private Equity led an investment in Home Instead Holding (today Dovida), a Rheinfelden-based international provider of non-medical home care, alongside founder Paul Fritz and Swiss family office Verium. The platform supports seniors and other clients in living independently at home across Europe and Australasia.

Key facts
  • Founded in Switzerland in 2007 (formerly Home Instead)
  • Approx. 12,000 caregivers across the network
  • Presence in Switzerland, Ireland, Australia, the Netherlands and further markets
  • Services include companion care, home help, overnight and dementia care
Sources
  • Private Equity Wire — Unigestion leads Home Instead buyout
  • Unigestion — company profile & investment background
  • Ardian — Dovida press release (company facts)

Industrial Turnaround

Gustav Wolf

Acquired by CMP

2021TurnaroundIndustrial

In December 2021, CMP Capital Management-Partners (via German Opportunity Fund III) acquired 100% of Gustav Wolf GmbH, a Gütersloh-based manufacturer of steel wire ropes for elevators and cranes founded in 1887. The investment supported an operational repositioning and further capital for European market-share growth.

Key facts
  • 100% share acquisition by CMP / GOF III (Dec 2021)
  • Approx. 500 employees across plants in DE, HU, PL, CN and the US
  • Annual turnover of more than EUR 80 million at announcement
  • Ninth investment of CMP’s German Opportunity Fund III
Sources
  • Gustav Wolf / CMP — press release (PDF)
  • Lift-Journal — CMP takes over Gustav Wolf
Acquired by
Healthcare Buy-and-Build

Ortivity

Acquired by Unigestion

2025Buy-and-BuildHealthcare

In 2025, Unigestion Private Equity joined Apheon as a capital partner in Ortivity, Germany’s leading outpatient orthopaedic care platform. An approximately EUR 200 million capital injection financed continued cluster expansion under a physician-led partnership model, with Apheon remaining lead investor and Unigestion taking a significant minority stake.

Key facts
  • Founded in 2022 by leading physicians together with Apheon
  • More than 100 medical centres across regional clusters in Germany
  • Approx. EUR 200m capital injection; Unigestion as significant minority partner
  • Physician partnership model with roughly half of ownership held by physicians
Sources
  • Apheon — Unigestion joins Ortivity
  • Taylor Wessing — capital investment by Unigestion
Acquired by
Industrial Buyout

SICIT

Acquired by Schroders Capital

2025BuyoutIndustrial

In May 2025, Renaissance Partners and TPG Rise Climate signed a binding agreement to jointly acquire control of SICIT Group S.p.A., a Chiampo (Vicenza)-based global leader in biostimulants produced from leather-industry residues under a circular model. Schroders Capital participated as a major co-investor alongside LGT Capital Partners, with Intesa Holding retaining a minority stake.

Key facts
  • Co-control partnership: Renaissance Partners and TPG Rise Climate
  • Schroders Capital and LGT Capital Partners as major co-investors
  • Circular model converting tannery by-products into agri-inputs and related products
  • Export-led B2B franchise serving agrochemical customers worldwide
Sources
  • TPG — binding agreement to acquire control of SICIT
  • Business Wire — joint acquisition announcement
  • Oaklins — SICIT transaction overview

SABSEG Group

Acquired by Schroders Capital

2026Buy-and-BuildBusiness Services

Schroders Capital deepened its minority investment in SABSEG Group alongside majority shareholder Miura Partners as part of a shareholder recapitalisation supporting the platform’s Southern European buy-and-build strategy in specialised insurance brokerage for SMEs and the middle market.

Key facts

  • Network of ~2,000 agents and 60+ offices; ~350,000 clients
  • More than EUR 800m gross written premiums in 2025
  • Growth plan targeting EUR 2bn GWP by 2030 with >EUR 350m committed capital
